Sleep
- The menu pauses its music and stops drawing before the console sleeps, so the console can really sleep instead of only turning the screen off.
- The logs now say whether the console really slept.

Controllers
- A single Joy-Con held sideways works in every menu. GameCube and other external controllers can drive HOME.

Display
- Docking and undocking with HOME open resizes the picture at once. HOME drops to 30 fps when idle.

Music and lock screen
- Music picks up where it stopped when you come back from a game (Theme Shop > Sounds).
- Lock screen transitions in Settings > Sleep.

Themes and art
- Themes copied to switch/SwitchU/themes show up under Installed.
- SteamGridDB background quality and blur; posters for 2x3 tiles.
