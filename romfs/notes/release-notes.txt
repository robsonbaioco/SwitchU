Internet in the menu works again
- Since 2.11.0 the automatic clock sync held the console's socket service, so the Theme Shop, the update check, SteamGridDB and Send logs all failed with "no connection" while Wi-Fi itself worked. Both now share one socket runtime.

Icon
- The SwitchU Menu entry uses the color-palette icon again.
