Fix
- 2.13.1 did not boot (error 2128-0100): the daemon's heap must be a multiple of 2 MB. 2.13.2 uses 2 MB, still 6 MB less than before 2.13.1, and keeps everything else from 2.13.1.

Controllers
- New Test Vibration in Settings > Controllers: every connected controller vibrates for half a second and the message lists the players that accepted the command.
